# Applied tasks and project portfolio: practical

Allow 90 minutes for this portfolio workshop after attempting the four ALT contexts. Deliver an index linking evidence from each context, a requirement-to-test table, a contribution record and an individual reflection. The reference templates in `code/portfolio/` are planning aids for classroom practice, not a completed SEC report.

Choose one of your practice artefacts to improve in this workshop. State its user need in two sentences and select three requirements that can be demonstrated. Explain what you implemented yourself and what came from an example or teammate. All datasets should remain fictional or appropriately non-personal. Success is an organised, honest account that another learner can inspect and run.
