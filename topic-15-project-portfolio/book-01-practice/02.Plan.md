# Plan two tests

Connect two requirements to expected results.

1. Write two observable requirements for your artefact.
2. Copy this table and replace the example with your own two rows.

| Requirement | Input/setup | Expected result | Evidence to keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean is correct | Readings `[4,6,8]` | Mean 6 | Output and calculation `(4+6+8)/3` |
| Empty input is handled | Empty readings | Whatever your stated contract requires | Output/error and explanation |

3. Run the checks. Record actual results. Choose **one** small defect or improvement. If both pass, try a boundary case or improve an unclear message; do not add a large new feature.
4. Predict the result that would show your change worked. Save a copy of the current version.

**Checkpoint:** two tests have expected and actual results; one change is small enough to finish next session. Continue to **Improve**.

**Optional team task:** assign developer and tester roles, then swap. Each person records one specific contribution. **Optional full-portfolio task:** list the four ALT contexts and link existing evidence. Mark missing contexts “next to practise” and label simulated hardware work.
