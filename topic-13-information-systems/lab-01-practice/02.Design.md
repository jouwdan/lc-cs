# Draw the data and interaction

Draw two tables, Book and Category, identify their primary keys and show the foreign-key relationship. Predict the rows returned by an empty search and by a title substring. Explain why a category name belongs in the category table rather than being copied inconsistently into many records.

Sketch the first-load, loading, success, no-match and error states. Label the search field and give the button an action-oriented name. Decide what information belongs in each result and how availability is communicated without colour alone. Trace the planned route from form submission through HTTP and SQL back to the DOM. **Higher Level:** compare two wireframes against the same criteria and justify the chosen design using observations from a partner task walkthrough.
