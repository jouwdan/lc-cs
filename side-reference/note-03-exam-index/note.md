## Marking reminders

- Match the year, level and question. A scheme may accept other correct answers. Ask a teacher about uncertain alternatives; do not turn a practice score into a future-grade prediction.
- Write outputs in execution order with the required line breaks. See 2025 Higher Q1 and 2024 Ordinary Q1.
- Show requested trace rows and sorting passes, not just the final answer. See 2021 Higher Q5/Q14 and 2025 Ordinary Q11.
- If asked to name **and explain**, connect the named component, principle, role or risk to the scenario. See 2025 Higher Q12/Q13 and 2024 Ordinary Q14.
- For social effects, explain benefits and risks for named people, with reasons. See 2020 Higher Q14, 2021 Higher Q15, 2023 Higher Q13 and 2024 Higher Q13.
- Section C schemes can reward partial progress using year-specific scales. Save working attempts and consult that question's descriptors; there is no single partial-mark rule for every year.
- Put a once-per-string score adjustment outside a character loop when the requirement applies only once. See 2020 Higher Q16(a)(vi).
- Analyse the supplied algorithm. The fixed-pass bubble sort in 2024 Higher Q14 is quadratic even when already sorted; an early-exit version is a different algorithm.
- For 2025 Higher Q16(b), handle empty, odd and even lists and sort before finding a median. That task prohibits a library median function; follow its own instructions.
