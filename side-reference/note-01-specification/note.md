# Curriculum and course coverage

The three strands are taught together: principles, core concepts and applied practical work.

This course uses the legacy specification applying to the 2020–2025 papers. Curriculum Online identifies it as the specification for examination through June 2028; the replacement curriculum is outside this course's claim of coverage. Teaching summaries are original paraphrases, not substitutes for the official wording.

| Official outcome group | Where to study | Evidence to produce |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1–1.10: computational problem solving | 05, 08, 11 | Decomposition, state trace, algorithm and justified choices |
| 1.11–1.18: society, history, AI, usability, careers | 02, 04, 13, 14 | Stakeholder analysis, accessible design and evidence-based evaluation |

## Level distinctions

Shared core work supports both levels. Higher Level extensions include heuristics, Turing machines, detailed CPU components, combined gates, protocol depth, quicksort, recursion, efficiency, aspects of testing, relational database depth and agent-based emergence. The official specification uses bold words and clauses to distinguish requirements: an entire topic is not automatically Higher Level only. Optional enrichment is labelled separately when it goes beyond the required core.
