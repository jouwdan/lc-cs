# Reflect and plan

Use four sentences to choose your next task.

1. “I expected …”
2. “The result showed …”
3. “I changed … because …”
4. “Next time I will test …”

**Example:** “I expected my loop to stop at zero. The trace showed the count never changed. I added the missing update because the exit condition depended on that count. Next time I will test starting at zero and at one.”

Add only what fits your work: your contribution to a team; one assumption in a model; one keyboard-access check; or the exact exam question reference. Keep the first attempt so you can see the improvement.
