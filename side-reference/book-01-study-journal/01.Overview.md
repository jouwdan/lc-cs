# Study journal

Keep one useful note after each session.

Spend **two minutes** after a session. Keep your own copy on paper or in a private document. You do not need a long report or an entry for every page.

1. Copy the blank row below.
2. Name one thing you tried without help.
3. Record one result and one small next step.

| Date and topic/question | What I tried | What I learned from the result | My next step and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Example: Topic 08, selection sort | Traced `[6,3,8,2]` | I skipped the unchanged second pass | Trace a new list tomorrow, recording every pass |
| Your entry | … | … | … |

For exam work, include year, level, section and question. For code, keep a test record on the next page. For teamwork, add one sentence saying what you contributed. Leave real personal data out of public course files.

**Checkpoint:** your next step is small enough to do, not just “revise more”. Continue to **Test record**, or return to <a href="https://tutors.dev/course/lc-cs">the course</a>.
