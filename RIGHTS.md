# Sources and rights

The examination papers and marking schemes in `topic-17-past-papers` are unchanged third-party documents sourced from Better Exams, which identifies the State Examinations Commission as their source. All original SEC and third-party copyright notices remain in the PDFs. Their availability for download does not make them public domain. No licence for this repository grants rights to those documents, their illustrations, or other embedded third-party material.

The Markdown lessons, questions, practice code, diagrams and synthetic datasets were newly authored as educational study material. They are not official SEC materials, endorsed exam answers or submissions for a live coursework brief. No blanket software/content licence is asserted for the repository. External video and audio remain hosted by their creators and retain their rights; course activities link/embed those sources rather than copying recordings.

`exams/manifest.json` records each downloaded URL, source landing page, metadata, byte size, page count and SHA-256 digest. `exams/coverage.json` contains original concise skill descriptions and page references, not reproductions of paper text. Retrieval and curriculum verification took place on 3 November 2026.
