# Your study route

Follow these links in order. Split the topic over several short sessions.

1. <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/note-01-learn"><strong>Learn</strong></a> — read an example and try a small question.
2. <a href="https://tutors.dev/lab/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/book-01-practice"><strong>Practise</strong></a> — follow the lab steps and check your results.
3. <a href="https://tutors.dev/quiz/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/quiz-01-check"><strong>Quiz</strong></a> — try the quiz with your notes closed.
4. <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/note-02-feedback"><strong>Answers</strong></a> — explain one correction.

Then <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/note-03-exam-practice">try one exam question</a> or <a href="https://tutors.dev/talk/lc-cs/topic-16-exam-preparation/talk-01-recap">review the recap slides</a>.

**Short of time?** Do one lesson section and its example today. The extra resources below are there when you need them.
