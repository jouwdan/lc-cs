# Mark, diagnose and retry

Use the exact matching scheme or the original mock's educator guide. Record marks by subpart and identify the evidence that earned them. Flag uncertain alternative answers for teacher discussion. For every lost mark, classify the cause: missing knowledge, misconception, incomplete explanation, calculation, code defect or time management.

Choose three repairs. Write a corrected explanation, test a boundary case or complete a small new example. After a delay, attempt a different question on the same skill without looking at the correction. Compare the new work with the original gap. Submit both attempts and a short reflection explaining which change in your study process helped. Success is improved independent reasoning, not merely copying a correct answer into the first script.
