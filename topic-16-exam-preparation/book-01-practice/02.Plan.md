# Translate instructions into a time budget

Read the cover and instructions, list mandatory and optional questions, note marks and duration, and record permitted materials. Create a budget that includes reading and checking. For the 2025 Higher Level A/B paper only, try 5 minutes selecting, 32 minutes A, 45 minutes B and 8 minutes checking. For its 60-minute C paper, try 5 minutes inspecting, 40 coding, 10 testing and 5 verifying saved files.

Predict which topics may need care without opening the scheme. Prepare blank trace tables and a test log if allowed in your practice conditions. Decide in advance how you will move on from a stalled subpart while preserving evidence of partial progress. Start with the paper's instructions, not a memorised pattern from another year.
