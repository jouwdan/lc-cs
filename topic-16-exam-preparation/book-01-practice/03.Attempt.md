# Work independently and preserve evidence

Attempt the selected questions without notes under the chosen conditions. For written work, underline command words and label every subpart clearly. For code, save the required file names, make one change at a time and record the tests you actually ran. Trace unexpected output before rewriting a large section.

At your planned checking point, review missing parts, loop boundaries, units, output formatting and files. Keep the uncorrected attempt when time ends. If you need to continue for learning, label later work as untimed. That distinction helps identify whether the gap was understanding, implementation or speed. Do not turn a practice score into a claim about ability without considering the conditions and evidence.
