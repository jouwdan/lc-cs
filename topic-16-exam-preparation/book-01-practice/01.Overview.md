# Exam preparation and synthesis: practical

Allow two sessions: one for a timed attempt and one for marking and repair. Choose an archived paper or the original mock. Deliver your first attempt, a marking record, an error log and a delayed retry. If using an archived paper, write its year, level, section and printed instructions at the top of your work.

For Section C, ensure required starter code is available before starting the clock. Better Exams lists PDFs, not every official `.py` starter. Obtain the files through your teacher or reconstruct printed code carefully for practice, checking indentation against the PDF. Do not mistake this course's original solutions for official starter programs.
