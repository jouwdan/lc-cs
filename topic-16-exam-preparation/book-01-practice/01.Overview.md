# 2. Practise: Exam skills

Try one question and repair one gap.

**Core: two 25–30 minute sessions**, plus a short retry later. You need a document or paper, a timer and the linked PDF. Start with one question, not a whole paper.

1. Choose a route from the table. You can choose a different topic question if it targets a known gap.
2. Open the paper only. Keep the scheme closed.
3. Write the year, level, section and question at the top of your work.
