# Mark and retry

Find one gap, repair it and retry later.

1. Open the matching scheme from the **Overview** table. Check its year, level and question.
2. Mark one subpart at a time using its allocation. Underline evidence in your answer. Keep uncertain alternative answers for a teacher to check.
3. Complete one row:

| Question/part | Marks earned/available | Specific gap | Small repair | Retry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Your reference | Use the scheme | E.g. skipped intermediate swaps | Trace one new list after every swap | Fresh sorting question in two days |

4. Do the repair now. Keep it separate from the first attempt.
5. After a day or two, attempt a fresh question without notes. Use a suggested route below or the <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/side-reference/note-03-exam-index">full index</a>. Choose only the subparts that practise your repaired skill, and read their exact wording.

| Skill | Fresh question | Matching scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Trace a sorting algorithm | [2024 Higher Q14](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jouwdan/lc-cs/main/topic-17-past-papers/unit-2024/tutorial-exam-paper-section-aandb-higher-level-ev/exam-paper-section-aandb-higher-level-ev.pdf#page=15) (choose the sort-trace subparts; this is a Higher Level extension) | [Scheme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jouwdan/lc-cs/main/topic-17-past-papers/unit-2024/tutorial-marking-scheme-higher-level-ev/marking-scheme-higher-level-ev.pdf#page=17) |
| Trace and explain program state | [2024 Ordinary Q13](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jouwdan/lc-cs/main/topic-17-past-papers/unit-2024/tutorial-exam-paper-section-aandb-ordinary-level-ev/exam-paper-section-aandb-ordinary-level-ev.pdf#page=11) (choose a loop or search subpart) | [Scheme](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jouwdan/lc-cs/main/topic-17-past-papers/unit-2024/tutorial-marking-scheme-ordinary-level-ev/marking-scheme-ordinary-level-ev.pdf#page=9) |

**Done:** the record shows what you could do first, what you changed and whether the skill transferred. If the new attempt still needs help, make the repair smaller and repeat. A copied correction is not an independent retry.

**Optional full-section/mock route:** keep a row for each lost-mark pattern and choose three repairs. Use the mock's own guide only for the course mock; it does not mark an SEC paper.
