# Logic

Make all eight rows for C, B and S. Add intermediate columns B OR S and C AND (B OR S). Outputs in row order 000,001,010,011,100,101,110,111 are 0,0,0,0,0,1,1,1.

Compare the output of (C AND B) OR S. Rows 001 and 011 expose the difference. Write the original user rule in words and identify which expression meets it. Draw labelled gates on the accompanying whiteboard, then check your drawing against the table.
