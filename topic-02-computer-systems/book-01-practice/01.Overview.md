# 2. Practise: Systems

Draw a computer and trace its instructions.

Draw a simple loan station and explain what each part does. No code or special software is needed.

**You need:** paper and the <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-02-computer-systems/note-01-concepts">lesson&#x27;s component tables</a>. **Time:** two sessions of about 25 minutes.

A visitor scans a book. The computer checks it, saves the loan and displays a due date. Use this same example throughout.

1. **Design:** draw and label the parts.
2. **Trace:** follow four instructions.
3. **Evaluate:** choose hardware and explain two OS jobs.

**Done when:** you have one labelled diagram, a completed trace and two short explanations. Work alone or discuss with a partner, then write your own answers.
