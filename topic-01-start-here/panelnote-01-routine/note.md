# Your study route

Start here. No coding experience is needed.

1. <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/note-01-concepts"><strong>Learn</strong></a> — read an example and try a small question.
2. <a href="https://tutors.dev/lab/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/book-01-practice"><strong>Practise</strong></a> — follow the lab steps and check your results.
3. <a href="https://tutors.dev/quiz/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/quiz-01-check"><strong>Quiz</strong></a> — try the quiz with your notes closed.
4. <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/note-02-feedback"><strong>Answers</strong></a> — explain one correction.

Then <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/note-03-exam-practice">try one exam question</a> or <a href="https://tutors.dev/talk/lc-cs/topic-01-start-here/talk-01-recap">review the recap slides</a>. You can leave exam questions until Topic 02.

**Short of time?** Do one lesson section and its example today. The extra resources below are there when you need them.
