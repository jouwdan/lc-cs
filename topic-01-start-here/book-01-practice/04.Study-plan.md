# Study plan

Choose three 25-minute sessions this week. In each, spend five minutes retrieving yesterday's learning, fifteen applying one concept and five checking work. Include one older topic in the next week's plan.

Peer review: can another learner follow your run instructions and understand your error correction? A complete submission contains the diagnostic including mistakes, restored program, error log and study plan. Success means a repeatable process, not four correct diagnostic answers. Reattempt the diagnostic from memory after a week.
