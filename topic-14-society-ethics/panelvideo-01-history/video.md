# Early computing: a guided viewing

CrashCourse Computer Science episode 1, produced with PBS Digital Studios. Optional enrichment with a written alternative in the society concept note.

Before viewing, predict why people wanted to automate calculations. During viewing, choose one device and record its input, process and output. Afterwards explain one social need that influenced its development. Distinguish a special-purpose device from a programmable general-purpose machine. Use captions where available.

Alternative: use the history section of Concepts and answer the same questions without video. The linked video is the creator's work; it is streamed, not redistributed.
