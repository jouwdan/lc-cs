# Make a recommendation

Write and check five clear sentences.

1. Choose **try a limited pilot**, **redesign first** or **reject for now**. Different decisions can be well supported.
2. Write five sentences using these prompts:
   - I recommend …
   - A benefit for … is … because …
   - One risk is … because …
   - Reduce that risk by …; this helps because …
   - I would change my decision if …
3. Underline facts supplied in the scenario. Circle assumptions you added. Revise any claim that sounds more certain than the evidence allows.
4. Say how a reader can challenge a poor recommendation, and who can change or override it.

**Done:** keep the stakeholder needs, three-row evidence table and five sentences. A strong answer links a feature to a person and a consequence. Compare with the <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-14-society-ethics/note-02-feedback">worked practice response</a> after writing your own.

**Optional extension:** compare two emerging technologies from Learn. Give each one's mechanism, one useful application and one limitation. Ask a partner to challenge one assumption; if studying alone, write the strongest objection yourself.
