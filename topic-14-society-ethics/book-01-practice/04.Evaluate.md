# Make a defensible recommendation

Write 250–350 words recommending adoption, a limited pilot, redesign or rejection of the fictional proposal. Include one concrete benefit, two distinct risks, a proportional mitigation, a limitation in the available evidence and a condition that would change your decision. Explain who can challenge a recommendation and how a human reviewer would make a meaningful decision.

Exchange reports. A partner should underline every claim supported by scenario evidence and circle every assumption. Revise unsupported certainty. Finish with a short comparison of two emerging technologies: describe their different mechanisms and one suitable use for each. Submit the evidence table and revised argument together, showing how feedback changed your reasoning.
