# Machine or test?

Distinguish two Turing ideas, then trace.

## Core — five minutes

1. Read the Turing definitions in <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-14-society-ethics/note-01-learn">1. Learn</a>.
2. Complete: “A Turing machine is …; a Turing test concerns …”.
3. Choose one development from the history table and explain one capability it made possible.

**Checkpoint:** your first sentence distinguishes a computation model from a conversational test. Your second links a development to an effect. Continue to **Evaluate**.

## Optional Higher Level trace — 15 minutes

1. Copy the two transition rules from Learn. Number tape cells from zero.
2. Trace `11_`, starting in `scan` at cell 0. Record head position, symbol read, symbol written, movement and next state for every step.
3. Repeat for `_`, the unary representation of zero. The tape also has blank cells to its left, so a left move is allowed.
4. Explain why both inputs halt and why you cannot apply these rules to an unexpected symbol.

**Expected nonblank tapes:** `111` and `1`. The blank rule writes one symbol and enters `halt`.
