# Computing, society, AI and history: practical

Allow 60–75 minutes. No external AI account or real personal data is required. A fictional school proposes a system that recommends library books from reading history. It reports “95% accuracy” without explaining its test set. Deliver a stakeholder map, evidence questions, a small machine trace and a reasoned recommendation.

Identify at least four stakeholders, including a learner who is poorly represented in the data. List possible benefits and concrete failure consequences. Distinguish automated rules from learned predictions. State what information you would need before accepting the performance claim. Success is a justified decision supported by mechanisms and evidence, not choosing a predetermined pro- or anti-technology position.
