**Diagnosis claim:** the overall percentage may hide missed rare cases. A missed case could delay help; a false alarm could cause worry or unnecessary follow-up. Ask for separate error counts and representative tests.

**Sensor checkpoint:** local processing is edge computing. The device still needs software updates, security and maintenance.

**Library practice — one possible recommendation:** “Try a small pilot. Readers may find books faster because the app narrows the catalogue. New readers may receive poor suggestions because their history is sparse. Let them choose interests and keep a normal search option. I would expand the pilot only after testing new-reader results and agreeing how records are deleted.” A librarian should be able to review and change a recommendation after a reader reports a problem.
