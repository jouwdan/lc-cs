## Marking guidance

- Use the scheme for the same year, level and question. Its examples are not exhaustive, and official examiners apply trained judgement. Course scores are formative; past rubrics do not predict future allocations. Evidence: All English marking schemes, PDF page 2.
- Write each requested output in execution order with the same line breaks as the program. Correct values alone can lose credit if layout changes meaning. Evidence: 2025 Higher Q1, scheme PDF page 4; 2024 Ordinary Q1, scheme PDF page 4.
- Show intermediate trace rows and sorting passes, not only the final answer. Schemes often recognise individual correct rows or steps. Evidence: 2021 Higher Q5 and Q14; 2025 Higher Q14(a)(ii); 2025 Ordinary Q11.
- When asked to name and explain, give a specific component, principle, role or risk and then connect it to the supplied scenario. Naming alone may satisfy only part of the rubric. Evidence: 2025 Higher Q12 and Q13; 2024 Ordinary Q14.
- Balanced discussion needs relevant benefits and drawbacks, with reasons and affected stakeholders. It is more than a list of fashionable technologies. Evidence: 2020 Higher Q14(c); 2021 Higher Q15(c); 2023 Higher Q13(c); 2024 Higher Q13(c).
- Section C uses graded response scales in which partial progress can receive credit; save an executable attempt and complete independently achievable requirements. Scale labels and thresholds vary by year. Evidence: English marking schemes, Section C scale tables on PDF page 3 and question-specific descriptors.
- Keep whole-string score adjustments outside a character loop when they should apply only once. Evidence: 2020 Higher Q16(a)(vi): scheme explicitly withholds full credit for placing the solution inside the character loop.
- Analyse the algorithm actually supplied. The fixed-pass bubble sort in 2024 is quadratic in both best and worst cases; early termination is an improvement, not an assumption about that code. Evidence: 2024 Higher Q14(c).
- For median code, sort first and handle empty, odd-length and even-length lists separately; the 2025 task prohibits a library median function. Evidence: 2025 Higher Q16(b), paper PDF pages 8–9 and corresponding scheme.
