# Verify, validate and report

Run the hand-trace test and boundary tests. Check that the final queue equals total arrivals minus total served and that no history value is negative. Deliberately pass probability 1.2 and confirm a clear error appears. Explain what the deterministic extreme tests establish and what they cannot establish.

Write a 200-word report containing your prediction, actual results, one mechanism explaining them, two assumptions and one useful real observation. Distinguish verification from validation. A partner should be able to reproduce your results from the parameters and seeds alone. For an agent-based extension, describe agents, local rules and an emergent pattern, including whether updates are simultaneous or sequential. Submit evidence of one change and its effect, not only the final program.
