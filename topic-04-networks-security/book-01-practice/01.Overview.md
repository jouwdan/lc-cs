# 2. Practise: Networks

Draw a network and match threats to controls.

Draw a small network for a fictional school study-room booking service, then choose protections for it.

**You need:** paper and the <a href="https://tutors.dev/note/lc-cs/topic-04-networks-security/note-01-concepts">network lesson</a>. **Time:** two sessions of about 25 minutes. No code, special software or real network testing is needed.

1. **Trace a request:** draw the devices and explain how a page arrives.
2. **Assess threats:** match four problems to suitable controls.
3. **Review and recover:** explain what to check when something fails.

**Done when:** you have one labelled diagram and four clear threat/control explanations. Use fictional data. These tasks do not require real passwords, network scanning or other people's traffic.
