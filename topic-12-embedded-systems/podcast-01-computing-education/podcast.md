# Hardware and computing education

Optional listening: balenaPodcast, Episode 01, interview with Eben Upton. Connect practical hardware design to access to computing education.

Listen for a distinction between learning how computing works and merely using a computer for another subject. Identify one design constraint discussed by the speaker and explain how a hardware choice might address it. Separate the speaker's perspective from evidence you can test in your own project.

Written alternative: a low-cost classroom device still needs suitable inputs, outputs, documentation and reliable operation. Compare two possible designs for a light logger using those criteria. Explain how cost can affect access, and why a lower price alone does not guarantee suitability. This paragraph is an original discussion prompt, not a transcript of the episode.
